# Code of Conduct

AgentKit welcomes people of every background and experience level. Everyone
participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, or other project
spaces is expected to help make them respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with care. Be direct about a problem without attacking
  the person who raised it.
- Respect different experiences, identities, abilities, and levels of knowledge.
- Give constructive feedback, accept corrections, and take responsibility for
  mistakes.
- Respect privacy and boundaries. Ask before sharing someone else's personal
  information or private communication.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern are unacceptable.
Repeated disruption or ignoring a maintainer's request to stop is also a
violation.

## Report a concern

Contact the maintainer privately at
[alex+git@ciobanu.org](mailto:alex+git@ciobanu.org), the contact listed on the
[maintainer's profile](https://github.com/pavkam). Include what happened, where
it happened, and any relevant links or evidence. Do not post conduct reports or
identifying details in a public issue.

Reports will be handled with discretion. Information may be shared with people
needed to investigate or respond; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.
If a concern involves the maintainer, or you need help with abuse on GitHub, use
[GitHub's reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation
when needed. Responses depend on severity and repetition, and may include a
request to correct behavior, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent
ban. Serious violations may lead to immediate removal.

Anyone affected by an enforcement decision may ask for clarification or review
through the same private contact. Retaliation for reporting a concern or
participating in an investigation is itself a violation.
